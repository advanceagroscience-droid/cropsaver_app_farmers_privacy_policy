1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Cropsaver (Advance Agro Science) (“Cropsaver”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal and farm-related information when you use the Cropsaver Farmer Android mobile application (“Farmer App”).

Cropsaver Farmer empowers growers with live video and audio consultations with verified Crop Mentors and VIP Crop Specialists, AI-assisted crop symptom analysis and pesticide bottle verification, structured spray prescriptions with EU Export MRL/PHI safety checks, farm plot health tracking, local agri-service bookings (drone spraying, soil/water/petiole/residue lab tests), and the Cropvibes (Kisan Showcase) farming community feed.

Our Core Privacy Promise: We never sell your personal phone number, farm location, or prescription records to third-party data brokers. Your data is used strictly to deliver accurate agronomy care, secure bookings, and a safe farming community.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Request Them

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used only when you actively join a live video consultation with a Crop Mentor or scan a crop/pesticide bottle label. Never accessed in the background without an active call.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Used only during live Mentor video/audio calls or when you tap voice input.
  • Foreground Service (Camera / Microphone): Keeps your active live video/audio consultation connected smoothly if you briefly switch apps during a Mentor call, with a visible system notification.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Optional; used when you pin a farm plot for hyper-local weather-checked spray reminders and nearby agri-service scheduling.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send 1-hour weather-checked spray reminders, 5-day recovery follow-ups, VIP appointment reminders, and regional pest/disease outbreak alerts.
  • Zero-Permission Android Photo Picker: When uploading crop photos, recovery progress photos, or Cropvibes posts, we use Android’s privacy-preserving Photo Picker. We do not request broad storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Connect you with verified Crop Mentors and Admin-verified VIP Crop Specialists for scheduled or live consultations.
  • Generate localized spray prescriptions (English, Marathi, Hindi), check FRAC/IRAC resistance rotation, and calculate EU/Export MRL Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI).
  • Provide optional AI-assisted crop symptom screening and bottle-label verification using Google’s Firebase AI Logic (Gemini).
  • Process subscription activations and service bookings securely through Razorpay and UPI gateways.
  • Operate, moderate, and keep the Cropvibes community safe against spam, abuse, or unauthorized chemical promotions.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations, dispute resolution, and platform security requirements.
Agronomy Advisory Disclaimer: Recommendations, AI checks, and spray prescriptions provided through Cropsaver Farmer are agricultural guidance tools. Always verify registered CIBRC product labels, dosage instructions, weather conditions, and local agricultural regulations before application.

5. Cropvibes (User-Generated Content) & Sponsored Agri-Ads

Public Showcase Posts & Moderation

If you choose to publish a crop harvest photo, Before/After recovery slider, or comment on Cropvibes (Kisan Showcase), your display name, village/district, crop tags, and chosen photo become visible to other Cropsaver users. You may report abusive posts or block other users at any time directly inside Cropvibes. Cropsaver Admin actively moderates reported posts to remove spam or unapproved chemical promotions in accordance with Google Play’s User-Generated Content policy.

Direct Agri-Sponsor Ads

To keep agronomy tools affordable for farmers, the Cropvibes feed may display direct agriculture-related offers (such as drip irrigation, nurseries, soil labs, or crop insurance) managed by Cropsaver Admin. Every promotional card is clearly marked “Sponsored”. Ads are matched only by general crop category or district and are never inserted inside clinical Mentor spray prescriptions. We record aggregate impression and click counts without selling your personal contact details to advertisers.

6. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Sharing

We share limited data strictly with trusted infrastructure partners needed to operate the Farmer App:

  • Verified Crop Mentors & VIP Specialists: Receive your consultation request, selected crop, problem type, plot context, and prescription history strictly to advise you.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Firebase AI Logic): Provides encrypted cloud storage, secure authentication, abuse protection, and optional AI crop/label analysis.
  • LiveKit Cloud (WebRTC): Transmits real-time encrypted audio and video during live Mentor calls.
  • Razorpay / UPI Payment Partners: Processes subscription and consultation payments securely under PCI-DSS and NPCI guidelines.
  • Authorized Agri-Service Partners: When you book drone spraying or soil/petiole/MRL lab testing, your chosen crop, acreage, village/location, and contact details are shared solely to fulfill that order.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority.

7. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the Farmer App and our cloud servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Cloud Firestore access is protected by strict per-user security rules and Firebase App Check so other farmers cannot access your private consultations, bookings, or payment records.

We retain your account and prescription records while your account remains active so you can review seasonal crop history and MRL compliance logs. Payment and statutory billing records are retained as required by Indian tax and accounting laws.

8. Your Rights & Account / Data Deletion

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 and Google Play’s Data Deletion Policy, you have the right to access, correct, withdraw consent, or request permanent deletion of your account and personal data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
Send an email to advanceagroscience@gmail.com with the subject line “Cropsaver Farmer Account Deletion Request” along with your registered mobile number or sign-in email. Upon verifying ownership, we will permanently delete your profile, farm plots, recovery photos, and Cropvibes posts within 30 days (excluding minimal financial transaction logs strictly required by law).

9. Children’s Privacy

Cropsaver Farmer is intended for adult farmers and agricultural stakeholders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 18 without verifiable parental/guardian consent). If you believe a child has submitted personal data, please contact us immediately at advanceagroscience@gmail.com for prompt removal.
